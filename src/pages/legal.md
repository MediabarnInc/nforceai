At nForce AI, we are committed to maintaining the privacy and protection of your personal information. This privacy policy outlines how we handle information collected through our website.

## 1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal data from visitors to our website except for IP addresses via Google Analytics. The information collected through Google Analytics is entirely anonymous and is used solely for monitoring traffic and website performance.

## 2. Contact forms

Our website includes a contact form where you can provide your general contact information (such as your name, email and phone number) to reach out to us. However, we do not store or retain any of this data after responding to your inquiry.

## 3. Use of cookies and tracking technologies

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous traffic data, including IP addresses. This helps us improve our website and services by understanding how visitors interact with our site. No other cookies or tracking technologies are used.

## 4. Data storage and security

nForce AI does not store or retain any personal information. Any data submitted via the contact form is not stored on our servers, and all interactions are handled securely.

## 5. Data sharing

We do not share any personal data with third parties.

## 6. Opt-out

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no need for an opt-out process. If you have any concerns or questions regarding your data, contact us at [contact@nforceai.com](mailto:contact@nforceai.com).

## 7. Data retention

We do not retain any personal information. Any data submitted through our contact form is used solely for the purpose of responding to inquiries and is not stored.

## 8. Compliance

While we are not subject to specific geographic or regulatory privacy frameworks such as GDPR or CCPA, we are committed to ensuring the privacy of our website visitors and protecting any data that may be temporarily handled.

## 9. Collection of data from minors

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

## 10. Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy policy, contact us at [contact@nforceai.com](mailto:contact@nforceai.com).
